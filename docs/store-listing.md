Reference copy for the store submission. Category: Productivity.

## Short description (132 char limit)

Flags ChatGPT prompts likely to get an agreeable answer instead of an honest one. Runs on-device, never sent anywhere.

## Detailed description

How you ask ChatGPT something shapes the answer you get back, often more than people realize. This extension watches your prompt as you type and flags it when the phrasing is likely to pull the model toward agreeing with you instead of giving you an honest, independent answer.

It catches two different things:

- **Leading phrasing.** Asserting a stance as settled fact, citing what "everyone knows," presenting only one side of a story, and other patterns that make agreement the easy answer.
- **High-stakes actions.** Medication dosing, money transfers, and legal or financial signing get flagged regardless of phrasing, because a wrong answer there is expensive to undo.

Click the badge for a one-line tip. The extension never edits your prompt and never blocks sending. A flag is information, not a gate.

**Privacy:** everything runs on your device. The extension makes no network requests, stores nothing, and sends your prompt text nowhere, including to its own developer. Full privacy policy: [link].
